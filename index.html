Choose Your Comfort

Understanding different firmness levels can help you make a more informed choice.

Soft
Medium
Firm

Soft Comfort

Provides a plush, contouring feel that allows you to sink gently into the material. It typically conforms closely to body curves.

Suitable use cases: Guest beds, lightweight sleepers, or seating that prioritizes deep relaxation.

Important: We recommend visiting the showroom to test and confirm the actual feel before purchase.

Medium Comfort

Offers a balanced blend of support and softness. It does not sink too deeply but avoids feeling overly rigid.

Suitable use cases: Everyday use, master bedrooms, and general multi-purpose seating.

Important: We recommend visiting the showroom to test and confirm the actual feel before purchase.

Firm Comfort

Provides a highly supportive surface with minimal sinkage. Designed to maintain structural shape under pressure.

Suitable use cases: High-traffic seating, strong base support layers, and those who prefer a rigid resting surface.

Important: We recommend visiting the showroom to test and confirm the actual feel before purchase.

How To Choose Foam

Educational information to help guide your showroom discussion.

Density

Refers to the mass of the foam. Generally influences weight and feel. High density does not always mean firm.

Firmness

How the foam yields under pressure. Ranges from very soft to very firm depending on construction.

Thickness

The depth profile. Thicker foams often provide a different comfort dynamic than thinner sheets of the same material.

Usage

Consider what the foam will be used for—sleeping, sitting, or back support—as this changes the ideal specification.

Size

Ensuring the material will fit its intended space. We can discuss available cuts when you contact us.

Durability

General structural integrity over time. Varies widely based on product type, usage frequency, and environment.
